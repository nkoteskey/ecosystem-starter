# Open questions

Decisions that appear in the source documents but are **ambiguous, not yet
settled, or explicitly deferred** — so they do not yet warrant an accepted
record. Each entry cites its source. When one resolves, promote it to a
numbered record and strike it here with a link.

The discipline (see [`README.md`](./README.md)): **do not invent a decision
you cannot trace to a source.** These are the items where the source
itself says "to be decided", flags an inconsistency, or leaves a fork open.

Entry shape:

```
## Q<n>. <one-line title>

<What the source says, and why it is not yet a decision.>

- **Needs:** <what would resolve it — a fact to verify, a choice to make, a person to ask>.
- **Source:** <document §section, or file:line>.
- **Resolved:** (leave blank; fill with the record number and date when promoted)
```
