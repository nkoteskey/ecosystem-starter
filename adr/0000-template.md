## Context

The situation, the forces at play, and the problem this decision resolves.
Factual; the reasoning goes below. For a retrospective record, note when
the decision was originally made and cite the source.

## Decision Drivers

- The forces that pushed toward the outcome: requirements, constraints,
  non-negotiables, quality attributes. Each should be something an option
  can be scored against.

## Considered Options

1. **Option A** — one line.
2. **Option B** — one line.
3. **Option C** — one line.

For a retrospective record these must be the alternatives actually on the
table, not a strawman set.

## Decision

**Chosen: Option X.**

The decision in one or two sentences, then why this option won against the
drivers — and why each other option lost.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What gets better, easier or safer.

### Negative

- **What this decision costs.** Be concrete: the capability given up, the
  risk taken on, the complexity added, the door closed.

### Neutral

- Follow-on facts: new invariants to uphold, seams introduced, work now
  required elsewhere.

## Validation

How we know the decision is honoured: the tests, grep invariants, gates or
checklist rows that enforce it. For a retrospective record, the shipped
implementation that ratifies it.
